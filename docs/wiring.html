The colours show what a wire carries; use whatever wire colours you have. Each ⏚ symbol goes to one shared ground on the perfboard, and one wire takes that ground to pin 38. The two lasers of a kind share a board pin, but each laser has its own transistor and its own 1 kΩ.

Where everything sits

Seen from behind, the way you hold it. The camera faces away from you; the lasers and the projector cartridge slide in from the back.

Part pinouts

PN2222 transistor (Q1, Q2, Q4, Q5)

E to ground, B to the 1 kΩ, C to the laser's black wire. This is the Adafruit part. Some "2N2222" parts are C-B-E, so check the datasheet of what arrives.

IRLB8721 MOSFET (Q3)

G to pin 9 directly, plus 10 kΩ to ground. D to the 8.2 Ω resistor. S to ground. The metal tab is also the drain, so keep it off other wires.

Kill toggle MTS-102

The middle lug takes 5 V from pin 40. One outer lug feeds the lasers' red wires and the LED's +; the other outer lug stays empty. The lever joins the middle lug to the lug on the opposite side, so check with a meter which lug is live in your "on" position.

Board pins used

Seen from the top, USB end up. In the grip the USB end points down. On the ESP32-S3-Pico, confirm the GND pins against the silkscreen; any GND pin works.

IMU breakout (Adafruit LSM6DSOX): use only VIN, GND, SCL and SDA. LED star: the pads are marked + and −.

Before you power it

The line lasers are Class 3R. Never look into them, and point the scanner at a wall or a sheet of paper while testing.
  • Power budget. The LED draws about 340 mA and each laser a few tens of mA, so about 0.45 A in all with two lasers, and a little more with four. A USB 2.0 port is rated for 0.5 A. With four lasers, use a USB 3 port (0.9 A) or a powered hub.
  • First test with the toggle off. Plug in the board and send HELLO in a serial terminal; it should answer with imu=LSM6DSOX. Then switch the toggle on and try EMIT 20 0 0, EMIT 0 20 0 and EMIT 0 0 20. The firmware turns everything off 1.5 s after the PC goes quiet.
  • Keep the emitters off during reset (optional). A 10 kΩ resistor from each transistor base to ground keeps the lasers dark while the board is in reset or in boot mode.
  • Heat. The 8.2 Ω resistor dissipates about 1 W and gets warm. Leave it room on the perfboard, away from the plastic.
  • Wires to the head. Run them through the slot under the camera: the camera USB, one +5 V to the toggle, threefive returns (V, H, LEDV, V2, H, H2, LED) and four IMU wires. Sleeve each joint with heat-shrink.
